# Contributing

Every change to the platform is made by its maintainer, through the maintainer's own process. A
pull request from outside is read as a report of what its author needs and closed with its answer;
what it asks for is built when the roadmap takes it.

To report a problem or ask for a feature, open an issue saying what you did, what you saw and what
you expected; the bug report and measurement report templates are the forms.

Conduct: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). A security problem goes through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)'s private reporting, never a public issue.
